GPU Thermal Optimization

Hardware diagnostics reliability engineering preventive maintenance benchmarking

Investigated repeated system crashes occurring during moderate gaming workloads by running performance and temperature tests, which identified inadequate GPU thermal performance as the root cause.

Disassembled the graphics card, removed degraded thermal compound, and applied PTM7950 to restore effective heat transfer between the GPU die and cooling solution.

The intervention significantly reduced operating temperatures and resulted in a more stable and reliable system under sustained load.
