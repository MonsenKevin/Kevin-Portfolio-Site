GPU Thermal Optimization

Hardware diagnostics reliability engineering preventive maintenance benchmarking

Problem: High-end GPU running at unsafe temperatures under load, causing thermal throttling and system instability.

Approach: Ran stress benchmarks and monitored thermals to confirm GPU overheating as root cause. Disassembled the card, cleaned old thermal pads and paste, applied fresh Thermal Grizzly materials, and validated recovery with a repeat benchmark.

Outcome: Temperatures reduced to safe operating range. System stable under sustained load with documented before/after thermal profiles.
