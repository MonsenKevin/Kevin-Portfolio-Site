Cloud Email and Workspace Migration

Change management Google Workspace DNS IT documentation stakeholder communication

Problem: Scout organization running a legacy email platform that was unreliable, costly, and not manageable by non-technical volunteers.

Approach: Planned and executed a staged migration to Google Workspace with zero downtime, including DNS cutover, mailbox migration, and youth protection compliance validation. Produced runbooks and trained an adult leader for ongoing administration.

Outcome: 50+ users migrated successfully with no service interruption. Eliminated $200/year in fees and left the organization with a maintainable, documented system.
