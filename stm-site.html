Design Exercise

Usability professional design maintainability web development

Problem: Needed a design exercise to explore what a professional MSP firm's online presence should look like — usable, credible, and maintainable.

Approach: Designed and built a full professional services site with service pages, about section, and contact flow. Focused on clarity and long-term maintainability over visual complexity.

Outcome: Production-ready site deployed as a portfolio and design reference for future MSP work.
