AI Essay Feedback Tool

Full-stack development AI integration TypeScript rapid prototyping project leadership

Problem: Students lack access to immediate, structured feedback on essay quality between drafts.

Approach: Led a 3-person team through a hackathon sprint. Designed the UI in Google Stitch, implemented structured rubric-based feedback logic in TypeScript using the Google AI Studio API, and used Kiro as the primary AI development tool throughout.

Outcome: Working prototype delivered and presented to evaluators within the hackathon window.
