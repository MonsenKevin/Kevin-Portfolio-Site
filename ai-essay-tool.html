AI Essay Feedback Tool

Full-stack development AI integration TypeScript rapid prototyping project leadership

As a college student I had always found that the hardest part of essay writing was feedback. I could read my work over and over again but still not be satisfied with the writing but be at a loss on what to do. I also knew that many of my peers started their essays late at night, after the tutoring center was closed, therefore it was even more imparative for them to have access to a way to get feedback on their writing 24/7.

Many students have in recent years turned to artifical inteligence models to give feedback on their work but if done improperly can lead to the model writing the essay for them. This not only breaks acedemic integrety but hinders their growth as a writer.

Enter Midnight Tutor, my team's solution to this challenge. Students upload their essay, prompt, and rubric and get detailed feedback on where they can improve. It also has a brainstorming feature to help get the writing jucies flowing
